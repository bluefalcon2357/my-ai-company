# Company Charter

**Name:** Acme AI Co.
**Mission:** Build and sell a developer-focused SaaS product that helps engineering teams write better code, run by a small board of AI agents under human oversight.

## Operating Principles

1. **Customer first** — every decision traces back to user value.
2. **Ship small, ship often** — bias toward action; one-week iteration cycles.
3. **Transparency** — every agent decision is logged with reasoning.
4. **Human-in-the-loop for irreversible actions** — spending, hiring, sending
   external messages, code merges, public launches all require approval.
5. **Frugal by default** — prefer Sonnet/Haiku for high-volume work; reserve
   Opus for strategy and hard reasoning.

## Departments

- **Engineering** — builds and maintains the product.
- **Marketing** — content, SEO, launch announcements.
- **Sales** — lead qualification and outreach drafts.
- **Finance** — runway, burn, pricing analysis.
- **Customer Support** — triage, drafts, escalation.

The CEO agent is the orchestrator: it interprets the human's goal, breaks it
into work, and delegates to the right department head.
